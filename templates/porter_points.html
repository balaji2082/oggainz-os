Porter handoff points

Spots where the rider hands a far order to a Porter instead of riding it himself. Booking a Porter from the kitchen to Adyar is ~19 km; if the rider carries it to Sholinganallur junction — somewhere he passes anyway — the Porter only runs the rest. Same delivery, lower fare, no extra riding.

How a point gets used
  • An order under {{ 12 }} km on a corridor the rider is already riding — he delivers it.
  • A far order on a corridor he is riding — carried to the point here that serves that corridor, and a Porter is booked from there.
  • A far order on a corridor he is not riding at all — Porter booked direct from the kitchen, because a detour costs more of his time than it saves.

Pick a spot that is safe to stop at, easy to describe to a Porter driver, well lit after dark and next to a landmark. It only helps if it is on a route the rider already takes.
