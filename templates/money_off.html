The Money tab reads your Notion expense sheets and the revenue in this app, then builds the same dashboard you get on the laptop. It stays switched off until the server has a Notion token.

To switch it on: PythonAnywhere → Web tab → Environment variables → add OG_NOTION_TOKEN with your integration token (the same value as .notion_token on the laptop) → Reload.

Until then, use Edit-Dashboard.bat on the laptop — it works exactly as before, and your phone can reach it on home wifi.
