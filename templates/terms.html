Terms & Conditions

Meal subscriptions — simple, transparent, structured.

1Subscription validity

  • Monthly packs (20 meals) are valid for the number of days shown on your pack — 40 days (Signature) or 50 days (Elite / Athlete) from the start date.
  • Weekly packs (5 meals) are valid for 15 days from the start date.
  • Validity covers all planned usage, including skipped meals carried forward.

2Working days

  • Our kitchen operates Monday to Friday, plus select Saturdays based on availability and demand.
  • Sundays are non-operational.
If we're closed on a scheduled working day (public holiday, government regulation, or an operational reason), that day is not counted against your subscription — your validity extends automatically.

3Skip & carry forward

  • To skip a meal, inform us before 6:00 PM on the previous day.
  • Requests after this time cannot be accommodated — the meal is already in preparation.
  • Skipped meals carry forward and can be used within your validity period.
  • Extended usage beyond validity is a special case: request it before subscribing; approval depends on feasibility and is not guaranteed.

4Delivery & timing

  • Lunch: 11:00 AM – 1:45 PM  ·  Dinner: 5:30 PM – 8:00 PM.
  • Exact timing varies with route, location and order volume.
  • A specific delivery slot can be mutually scheduled in advance, subject to feasibility.
  • Please be available during your delivery window. If you're unavailable, the order is marked delivered; re-delivery is not guaranteed.
Where a delivery slot has been agreed and allocated, we will always do our best to deliver within that promised window. Even so, an occasional delay is possible — delivery depends on several factors beyond our control, such as traffic, weather and road conditions. Whenever a delay is expected, we will keep you informed. We appreciate your understanding.

5Delivery fee

  • Delivery is free within our free-delivery zone around our Perumbakkam kitchen.
  • For locations outside the free zone, a distance-based delivery fee applies — typically between ₹30 and ₹75 per delivery, depending on your location.
  • The applicable fee is confirmed with you at the time of booking and is shown as a separate line on your invoice, in addition to the subscription amount.
  • Once agreed, the delivery fee stays fixed for the duration of that subscription.

6Menu & customisation

  • The weekly menu shows the structure and variety of your plan. Individual items or days may change with ingredient availability and operational conditions.
  • Nutritional value and protein targets are always met — that's the promise that never changes.
  • Customisation is available within our listed options; last-minute changes may not be accepted.

7Payments, billing & refunds

  • All subscriptions are paid in advance; orders are confirmed only after payment.
  • Your invoice itemises everything clearly: the meal subscription (pack, duration and number of meals), any add-ons, the delivery fee (if applicable) and the total paid.
  • No refunds once a subscription has started. Any exception is at management's discretion.

8Quality & feedback

  • We genuinely want your feedback — it's how we improve.
  • Any issue with a meal must be reported on the same day of delivery so we can make it right.

9Nature of our service

  • OG Gainz is a subscription-based meal service, not an on-demand delivery platform. Meals are prepared in batches for consistency and quality.
  • Delays or changes can occur due to weather, supply-chain issues or unforeseen circumstances — we'll always keep you informed.

10Agreement

By subscribing to OG Gainz you agree to the terms above. Thank you for helping us deliver consistent, high-quality nutrition — every single day.
