01Subscription validity

  • Monthly packs (20 meals): valid for 40 days (Signature) or 50 days (Elite / Athlete) from the start date.
  • Weekly packs (5 meals): valid for 15 days from the start date.
  • Validity covers all planned usage, including skipped meals carried forward.

02Working days

  • Our kitchen operates Monday to Friday, plus select Saturdays based on availability and demand. Sundays are non-operational.
  • If we are closed on a scheduled working day (public holiday, government regulation or an operational reason), that day is not counted against your subscription — your validity extends automatically.

03Skip & carry forward

  • To skip a meal, inform us before 6:00 PM on the previous day. Requests after this time cannot be accommodated — the meal is already in preparation.
  • Skipped meals carry forward and can be used within your validity period.
  • Extended usage beyond validity is a special case: request it before subscribing; approval depends on feasibility and is not guaranteed.

04Delivery & timing

  • Lunch: 11:00 AM – 1:45 PM  ·  Dinner: 5:30 PM – 8:00 PM. Exact timing varies with route, location and order volume.
  • A specific delivery slot can be mutually scheduled in advance, subject to feasibility.
  • Please be available during your delivery window. If you are unavailable, the order is marked delivered; re-delivery is not guaranteed.
Where a delivery slot has been agreed and allocated, we will always do our best to deliver within that promised window. Even so, an occasional delay is possible — delivery depends on several factors beyond our control, such as traffic, weather and road conditions. Whenever a delay is expected, we will keep you informed.

05Delivery fee

  • Delivery is free within our free-delivery zone around our Perumbakkam kitchen.
  • Outside the free zone, a distance-based delivery fee applies — typically ₹30 to ₹75 per delivery, depending on your location.
  • The fee is confirmed at the time of booking and shown as a separate line on your invoice. Once agreed, it stays fixed for the duration of that subscription.

06Menu & customisation

  • The weekly menu shows the structure and variety of your plan. Individual items or days may change with ingredient availability and operational conditions.
  • Nutritional value and protein targets are always met — that promise never changes.
  • Customisation is available within our listed options; last-minute changes may not be accepted.

07Payments, billing & refunds

  • All subscriptions are paid in advance; orders are confirmed only after payment.
  • Your invoice itemises the meal subscription, any add-ons, the delivery fee (if applicable) and the total paid.
  • No refunds once a subscription has started. Any exception is at management's discretion.

08Quality & feedback

  • Any issue with a meal must be reported on the same day of delivery so we can make it right. We genuinely want your feedback — it is how we improve.

09Nature of our service

  • OG Gainz is a subscription-based meal service, not an on-demand delivery platform. Meals are prepared in batches for consistency and quality.

10Agreement

By subscribing to OG Gainz you agree to the terms above. Thank you for helping us deliver consistent, high-quality nutrition — every single day.
